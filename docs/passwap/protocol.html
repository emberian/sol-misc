why it matters

  • sell to a wallet you don't know. the buyer claims with words you sent privately.
  • the program can't hold funds hostage. each vault is a 2-of-3 of {program, maker, claimer}.
  • the rules are machine-verified. what's proved.

how it works

  • make: deposit token A. name the least token B you'll take for all of it; that's a rate, and 0 makes a free offer. pick a passphrase. posting costs 1000 DREGG.
  • claim: type the passphrase. take any amount up to what's left. pay at least the rate, or nothing if the offer is free. one transaction, all or nothing.
  • cancel: the maker takes back what's left, after any no-cancel-before time.
  • to buy instead: deposit the token you pay with. same rule, other direction.
  • partial claims keep the offer open. emptying it closes it. paying above the rate is fine.

the passphrase

loading…
  • never on chain. the chain sees a public key and a signature.
  • salted with the offer address: same words, different key per offer.
  • the claim key is public, so guessing is offline against it: about 10,000 guesses per second per GPU at these rounds.
  • the generator gives six words from the 2,048-word BIP39 list: 266, millions of GPU-years. five words from a 250-word list would be 240, about three GPU-years. use the generator.
  • shared privately. whoever has the words first can claim. lost? the maker cancels and re-offers.
  • export claim keypair on the claim page: your key as a file, for recovery.

guarantees

proved for every claim, cancel and make.

  1. no claim without the recorded key's signature.
  2. the maker gets the payment in full, and it clears the rate.
  3. the claimer gets exactly what they asked for, never more than the vault holds.
  4. the make fee is exactly 1000 DREGG; the take fee is exactly the rate on the payment, never more; a free claim pays none. only to the fee wallet.
  5. the program never moves a vault on its own signature.
  6. only the maker cancels, only after the no-cancel time, for exactly what's left.
  7. a closed offer admits nothing. no double claim.
  8. a claim that pays nothing is admitted only for a free offer.
  9. a legitimate claim, cancel or make is never refused.

trusted, not proved: the 300-line adapter, the SPL token programs, the runtime.

if the program disappears

  • the vault's owner is a plain SPL multisig: 2 of [program, maker, claim key]. the token program enforces it.
  • claimer: export the claim keypair. maker: your wallet keypair. vault authority and vault: claim page, under custody.
  • both sign one transfer:
spl-token transfer <MINT_A> <AMOUNT> <DESTINATION> --from <VAULT> --owner <VAULT_AUTHORITY> \
  --multisig-signer maker.json --multisig-signer passwap-claim-XXXXXXXX.json --fee-payer maker.json --fund-recipient
spl-token close --address <VAULT> --owner <VAULT_AUTHORITY> \
  --multisig-signer maker.json --multisig-signer passwap-claim-XXXXXXXX.json --fee-payer maker.json
  • different machines: --sign-only / --signer.
  • not recovered: the offer record's ~0.002 SOL rent.
  • alone, neither party can move the vault.
