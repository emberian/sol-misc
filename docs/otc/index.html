a passphrase-claimable escrow on Solana. one offer, one passphrase, one atomic swap. the maker can cancel any time before it's claimed.

no wallet connected
what happens when you claim your wallet pays the price to the maker's token account and receives the escrowed tokens in the same transaction, which must also be signed by the claim key your browser derives from the passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA512, 600,000 rounds, salted with the offer address). the passphrase never leaves this page. if the passphrase is wrong the transaction is refused on chain and nothing moves.
what happens when you create your tokens move into a vault owned by the offer account. the offer records the price, the two mints, and the claim key derived from the passphrase. anyone with the passphrase can claim by paying the price; you can cancel from this page and get the tokens back. the passphrase itself is never stored anywhere.
